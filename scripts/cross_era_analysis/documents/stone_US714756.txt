================================================================
PHYSICS REGIME: RADIATIVE / HERTZIAN WAVE (tuned broadcast)
================================================================
Stone's patent describes generating free, unguided, simple
harmonic electromagnetic waves from a vertical radiating
conductor and selectively receiving them with resonant tuned
circuits at distant stations. This is canonical tuned-radio
broadcasting — radiative far-field transmission.

================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================

The patent describes a method for transmitting wireless signals
using electromagnetic waves generated through forced harmonic
oscillations in vertical conductors. The innovation enables
selective communication between specific transmitting and
receiving stations by tuning apparatus to particular frequencies,
preventing interference from nearby stations operating at
different frequencies.

================================================================
SPECIFICATION HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================

Background problem:
Electromagnetic waves developed from vertical conductors
possessed superior transmission range compared to horizontal
conductor methods. However, existing systems lacked selective
transmission — signals reached all receivers indiscriminately.

Key solution:
The invention establishes "forced simple harmonic electric
vibrations" in elevated conductors at predetermined frequencies,
paired with resonant circuits at receiving stations tuned to
match those frequencies. This produces frequency-selective
wireless communication.

Technical principles:
Stone distinguishes between natural vibrations (circuits
restoring equilibrium after a disturbance) and forced vibrations
(externally imposed periodic oscillations). Resonant LC
circuits — condensers and inductance coils — achieve frequency
selectivity through impedance matching.

Acoustic analogy (paraphrased from the patent):
The transmitter corresponds to a tuning fork emitting a single
simple musical tone; the receiver corresponds to an acoustic
resonator capable of absorbing the energy of that single tone
only.

================================================================
APPARATUS
================================================================

Transmitting station:
- Alternating-current generator producing moderate-frequency
  current.
- Step-up transformer (spark coil) elevating voltage.
- Condenser charged to breakdown threshold.
- Spark gap initiating oscillatory discharge.
- Auxiliary inductance coil (L) providing impedance isolation.
- Primary / secondary transformer coils coupling energy to the
  elevated radiating conductor.

Receiving station:
- Vertical conductor receiving electromagnetic waves.
- Induction coil extracting vibratory EMF.
- Tuned resonant circuit (condenser-inductor configuration).
- Coherer responsive only to the resonant frequency.
- Telegraph relay or translating device actuated by the coherer.

================================================================
OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLE
================================================================

When electromagnetic waves impact a receiving antenna, induced
oscillations develop across a broad frequency spectrum. Only
energy matching the receiving circuit's resonant frequency
achieves maximum potential difference at the condenser terminals
and triggers the detection apparatus. Non-resonant frequencies
produce negligible voltage.

================================================================
CONSTRUCTION NOTES
================================================================

High-frequency coils should be designed so that "the potential
energy of the displacement currents in the coil shall be small
compared to the kinetic energy of the conduction current"
flowing through it. Recommended practice:
- Widely separated wire turns.
- Minimal wire insulation thickness.
- Air-core construction (no iron).
- Skeleton frames minimizing dielectric surface contact.
- Air-gap condensers (solid dielectrics avoided).

Multiple-station configuration:
Apparatus may share a single elevated conductor for several
stations operating at distinct frequencies. Substantial
auxiliary inductance in the coupling circuits "swamps the effect
of the mutual inductance" between circuits, preventing frequency
crosstalk.

================================================================
REPRESENTATIVE CLAIMS
================================================================

Claim 1 (paraphrased):
"The method of developing free or unguided simple harmonic
electromagnetic waves of a definite frequency, which consists in
producing forced simple harmonic electric vibrations of the same
frequency in an elevated conductor."

Claim 7 (paraphrased):
"The method of rendering a circuit resonant to a given high
frequency, which consists in balancing the reactance of an
air-condenser by the reactance of a coil, the amplitude of whose
potential energy is small compared to the amplitude of its
kinetic energy when it is supporting a current of said given
high frequency."

Claim 30 (paraphrased):
Independently developing forced harmonic vibrations at different
frequencies in elevated conductors while receiving the resulting
electromagnetic wave energies "each to the exclusion of the rest
in a separate circuit resonant to the same frequency."

Claims 34-38: Selective reception methods using multiple circuits
or multiple elevated conductors with auxiliary-inductance
specifications.

================================================================
NOTES ON PHYSICS (for structural discrimination experiment)
================================================================

Stone's system is the archetypal tuned-radio broadcast: "free or
unguided simple harmonic electromagnetic waves" explicitly
radiated from an elevated conductor, received by resonant
tuned circuits. The word "unguided" is itself a direct contrast
with Tesla's surface-guided / ground-conduction scheme. This
patent should classify cleanly as RADIATIVE / HERTZIAN.
