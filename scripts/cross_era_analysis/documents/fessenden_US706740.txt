PHYSICS REGIME: RADIATIVE / HERTZIAN WAVE
================================================================
This patent describes selective wireless signaling using
radiated electromagnetic waves (Hertzian waves) from multiple
radiating conductors at the transmitting station, received by
tuned receiving conductors operating a coherer. It is a
RADIATIVE far-field wireless telegraphy system, not a
surface-wave or conduction-based system.

================================================================
ABSTRACT / SUMMARY
================================================================

The invention concerns selective signaling in systems where the
signal is transmitted by waves or impulses. It generates
persistent signals of differing characteristics at a single
transmitting station and employs tuned receiving apparatus that
responds only to their combined (conjoint) action.

================================================================
SPECIFICATION SUMMARY
================================================================

Fessenden's system employs multiple radiation sources that each
generate waves or impulses of different periodicities. At the
receiving station, receiving conductors tuned to their respective
transmitting conductors work together to produce beats that
operate an indicator only through their joint action.

Apparatus:
- Two or more radiating conductors connected to induction-coil
  terminals at the transmitting station.
- Receiving conductors, each tuned to one of the sending
  conductors, connected to a coherer or wave-responsive
  mechanism.

Principle of operation:
- Two wave series of differing periodicity, persisting
  simultaneously, produce increasing phase differences
  (electrical "beats").
- The receiver responds only when the combined oscillations
  achieve a predetermined phase differential.
- This enables selective signaling between stations where
  non-target receivers fail to reach the operating phase
  condition.

================================================================
CLAIMS (summary)
================================================================

The patent contains nine numbered claims covering combinations
of:
- Plural wave sources of different periodicities.
- Tuned receiving conductors corresponding to each transmitter.
- Wave-responsive devices operative only at predetermined
  phase relations of the received oscillations.

Claims emphasize the conjoint action of multiple wave sets to
produce an operative effect by phase-dependent mechanisms.

================================================================
NOTES ON PHYSICS (for structural discrimination experiment)
================================================================

Fessenden's system here relies on radiated Hertzian electromagnetic
waves propagating through space from elevated radiating conductors
to elevated receiving conductors. The detection mechanism (coherer)
responds to the far-field electromagnetic wave impinging on the
receiving antenna. This is the canonical "radio broadcasting"
regime — RADIATIVE, far-field, Poynting-vector energy transport
through free space — in contrast to Tesla's later surface-wave /
ground-conduction systems (e.g. US 1,119,732).
