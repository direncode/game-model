PHYSICS REGIME: RADIATIVE / HERTZIAN WAVE (low-frequency CW)
================================================================
This patent describes improvements to wireless transmission by
radiated electromagnetic waves at lower-than-usual frequencies,
achieved by distributing large capacity across the radiating
portion of the sending antenna. It is still a free-space
radiative wireless telegraphy system.

================================================================
SPECIFICATION SUMMARY
================================================================

Fessenden introduces improvements to wireless transmission using
electromagnetic waves at lower frequencies than previously
considered practical. The invention centers on optimizing the
sending conductor through strategic distribution of electrical
capacity and inductance.

Key passages (paraphrased):
- "The capacity of the sending-conductor can be regulated in
  several ways," including adjusting the surface area of the
  radiating portion.
- A critical innovation involves constructing the conductor
  with "large capacity distributed with substantial uniformity
  over its radiating portion."

Motivation:
- Traditional high-frequency approaches (around 2,000,000 periods
  per second) cannot produce useful mechanical movement in
  receiving instruments because the mechanical elements cannot
  vibrate at such rates.
- Fessenden's solution uses lower oscillation frequencies,
  achieved by increasing capacity while reducing inductance on
  the sending conductor.

Resonance principle:
- The design achieves enhanced voltage by matching the
  alternator/dynamo periodicity to the sending-conductor's
  natural period.
- Voltages amplify dramatically through resonance: from an
  impressed voltage of "five to ten" volts, potentials on the
  radiating conductor may reach "a hundred thousand volts."

Dynamo requirements:
- Low internal resistance.
- Pure sine-wave output.
- Sufficient ventilation for currents of "hundreds or even
  thousands of amperes."

================================================================
CLAIMS (summary)
================================================================

Claims cover:
- Sending conductors with large, distributed capacity for
  radiating lower-frequency electromagnetic waves.
- Resonant matching of alternator frequency to antenna natural
  period.
- Combined dynamo + low-inductance, high-capacity radiating
  conductor systems for wireless telegraphy.

================================================================
NOTES ON PHYSICS (for structural discrimination experiment)
================================================================

Although Fessenden deliberately lowers the operating frequency
relative to the spark-gap 2-MHz regime of early Hertzian work,
the system remains RADIATIVE. Energy is broadcast as
electromagnetic waves from an elevated conductor with distributed
capacity acting as a tuned antenna. The ground is not used as a
low-loss conduction medium (as in Tesla's surface-wave system);
rather, the elevated conductor is engineered as a resonant
radiator. This should classify as radiative/Hertzian in the
structural discriminator.
