ITEM 1. BUSINESS

The Company designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The Company's customers are primarily in the consumer, small and mid-sized business, education, enterprise, and government markets. The Company sells its products and resells third-party products in most of its major markets directly to consumers, small and mid-sized businesses, and education, enterprise, and government customers through its retail and online stores and its direct sales force.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The Company is exposed to global economic conditions and supply chain disruptions. Material adverse changes in the economic conditions in any of the Company's major markets could harm demand for the Company's products and services. The Company is also subject to risks related to currency fluctuations, regulatory changes in major markets including data privacy and competition law, intellectual property litigation, and reliance on a limited number of suppliers and contract manufacturers concentrated geographically. Cybersecurity threats and information security incidents could compromise the Company's intellectual property and customer data.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Net sales for fiscal 2024 increased compared to fiscal 2023 driven by services growth and partially offset by lower iPhone net sales. Operating expenses increased due to higher research and development costs related to new product introductions. The effective tax rate was lower than the prior year due to favorable resolution of certain tax matters. The Company expects continued investment in research and development and capital expenditures.
