ITEM 1. BUSINESS

The Company is a global technology company that provides a wide variety of services and solutions to enterprises in industries including financial services, healthcare, government, telecommunications, retail, and manufacturing. ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The Company faces risks from currency fluctuations and changes in tax legislation in multiple jurisdictions. Cybersecurity attacks could disrupt operations and harm the Company's brand. The Company's services depend on third-party suppliers and partners. Industry consolidation among customers could reduce demand for the Company's services. Geopolitical tensions and trade policy changes could affect the Company's global operations.
