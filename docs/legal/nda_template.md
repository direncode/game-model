# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Founder-Friendly Template)

> **Not legal advice.** This is a starter mutual NDA suitable for
> early-stage technical disclosure conversations. Have a
> licensed attorney in your jurisdiction review and customize it
> before signing — particularly if the counterparty is a
> sovereign entity, government, or large corporation, in which
> case the counterparty will typically propose its own form.
>
> Replace bracketed `[fields]` with the actual values before
> sending. The counterparty will normally redline this document;
> the redlines are negotiable.

---

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship
involving the discussion, evaluation, or potential use of
proprietary technology developed by Party A relating to
structural-fingerprint data primitives, post-transformer training
paradigms, and related sovereign-grade data infrastructure (the
**"Purpose"**). To facilitate such discussions, each Party may
disclose to the other certain Confidential Information (defined
below).

### 2. Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any information disclosed by
one Party (the **"Disclosing Party"**) to the other (the
**"Receiving Party"**), in any form (oral, written, electronic,
visual, or otherwise), that is either (a) marked or identified
as confidential at the time of disclosure or shortly thereafter,
or (b) reasonably understood by the Receiving Party to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the
circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes,
without limitation:

- Source code, object code, technical specifications,
  algorithms, fingerprint constructions, score-vector
  derivations, operational primitives, training paradigms,
  architectural diagrams, deployment runbooks, and
  implementation details
- Business strategies, customer or partner identities, financial
  information, pricing models, sales pipelines, and product
  roadmaps
- Research data, performance benchmarks, evaluation results, and
  scientific findings
- Any information about third parties (customers, partners,
  investors) that the Disclosing Party is itself obligated to
  keep confidential

Confidential Information does **not** include information that
the Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written
records was: (i) publicly available at the time of disclosure or
became publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
(ii) already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure
without any obligation of confidentiality; (iii) independently
developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to
the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
(iv) rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality
restrictions.

### 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

(a) Use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely
for the Purpose;

(b) Hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and
exercise at least the same degree of care to prevent unauthorized
disclosure as it uses to protect its own confidential information
of similar sensitivity (and in no case less than reasonable
care);

(c) Limit access to the Confidential Information to those of its
employees, contractors, and professional advisors who have a
need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by written
confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those set
forth herein;

(d) Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software,
code, or technology disclosed under this Agreement, nor attempt
to derive any source code, algorithms, or trade secrets
therefrom, except as expressly permitted in writing by the
Disclosing Party;

(e) Not file or attempt to file any patent application that
incorporates, derives from, or is informed by the Disclosing
Party's Confidential Information without the Disclosing Party's
prior written consent;

(f) Promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing if the
Receiving Party becomes aware of any unauthorized disclosure or
use of the Confidential Information;

(g) Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, promptly
return or destroy all materials containing the Disclosing Party's
Confidential Information, and certify in writing that it has done
so (except that one archival copy may be retained for legal
compliance purposes, subject to ongoing confidentiality
obligations).

### 4. Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall
continue for a period of `[THREE (3)]` years thereafter, unless
extended by mutual written agreement. The Receiving Party's
confidentiality obligations with respect to any Confidential
Information disclosed during the term shall survive termination
for a period of `[FIVE (5)]` years from the date of disclosure;
provided that the Receiving Party's obligations with respect to
any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret
shall continue for so long as such information remains a trade
secret under applicable law.

### 5. No Grant of Rights

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to grant or shall be
construed as granting any license, ownership, or other right,
express or implied, in or to the Confidential Information,
including without limitation any patent, copyright, trade secret,
trademark, or other intellectual property right. All Confidential
Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing
Party.

### 6. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into
any further agreement, transaction, partnership, or business
relationship. Each Party may discontinue discussions at any time
without liability, subject only to the continuing obligations of
confidentiality set forth herein.

### 7. Trade-Secret Protection

The Parties acknowledge that the Disclosing Party's Confidential
Information may include information that constitutes "trade
secrets" within the meaning of the Defend Trade Secrets Act of
2016 (18 U.S.C. § 1836) and analogous state and foreign laws.
The Receiving Party agrees to treat any such information in a
manner consistent with applicable trade-secret law and
acknowledges that misappropriation thereof may give rise to
remedies including injunctive relief, statutory damages, and
attorneys' fees.

### 8. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, court order, or
governmental authority to disclose any of the Disclosing Party's
Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where
legally permitted, provide prompt advance written notice to the
Disclosing Party so that the Disclosing Party may seek a
protective order or other appropriate remedy. The Receiving
Party shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential
Information that it is legally required to disclose and shall
use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that any such
disclosure is afforded confidential treatment.

### 9. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that monetary damages may be inadequate
to remedy a breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing
Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including
injunctive relief and specific performance, in addition to any
other remedies available at law or in equity, without the
necessity of posting a bond.

### 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of the State of `[DELAWARE]` (or, if mutually
agreed, an alternative jurisdiction such as `[ENGLAND AND
WALES]` for international counterparties), without regard to its
conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively in
the state or federal courts located in `[DELAWARE]` (or such
other forum as the Parties may agree), and each Party hereby
consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

### 11. Entire Agreement; Amendment

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the
Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes
all prior or contemporaneous understandings, whether oral or
written. This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument signed by both Parties.

### 12. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full
force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision
shall be replaced by a valid and enforceable provision that most
closely approximates the intent and economic effect of the
original.

### 13. No Waiver

No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right
under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of such right;
nor shall any single or partial exercise of any right preclude
any other or further exercise thereof or the exercise of any
other right.

### 14. Counterparts; Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which
shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall
constitute one and the same instrument. Signatures transmitted
by electronic means (PDF, DocuSign, or similar) shall be
deemed original signatures for all purposes.

## Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending this NDA to a counterparty, verify:

- [ ] All `[bracketed fields]` filled in
- [ ] Effective date set
- [ ] Term lengths in §4 reviewed (3-year term + 5-year survival is
      industry standard for technical NDAs; sovereign / government
      counterparties often request shorter terms)
- [ ] Governing-law clause in §10 set to a jurisdiction acceptable
      to both parties (Delaware is US-friendly default; for UAE
      counterparties, English law is often acceptable as a neutral
      forum)
- [ ] Counterparty's legal name and address verified (use the
      legal entity, not the trade name; for UAE entities, use the
      "Company Limited" name as registered with the Ministry of
      Economy)
- [ ] You retain a signed copy in private storage

## When to use this NDA

**Use this NDA before disclosing:**
- Source code, architectural diagrams, deployment specifics
- The specific operational primitives and hand-back loop
  architecture (Provisional #02 subject matter — foreign rights
  preserved if undisclosed)
- The deterministic boundary layer specifics
- Customer or pipeline information
- Financial terms or negotiating positions

**You do not need this NDA before disclosing:**
- Material that is already on latentocean.com (high-level
  product description, sales one-pager content, public verticals)
- Material that is already in the public TCD-JEPA / Crystara
  repository
- The PRIMITIVE_SPEC.md (if/when that becomes public via the
  website)
- General business information already in the public domain

## After signing

Once executed:

1. Retain the signed copy in private storage (encrypted folder
   or password-protected cloud).
2. SHA-256 hash the executed PDF and OpenTimestamp the hash per
   `docs/patent/opentimestamps_procedure.md` — establishes
   cryptographic evidence of the executed-NDA date.
3. Log the NDA in a private register noting (a) counterparty,
   (b) effective date, (c) term, (d) date returned/destroyed,
   if applicable.

The executed NDA is itself a confidential document; do not
publish or share without consent of the counterparty.
